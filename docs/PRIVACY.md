# WinPebble PDF Page Duplicator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

WinPebble PDF Page Duplicator is a Windows utility that processes PDF documents
locally on your computer.

## Files you choose

The app accesses the PDF you select to read page dimensions and create a new
PDF. Selected documents and their filenames may contain personal information.
Processing takes place on your computer. The app does not upload your PDF files,
their contents, or filenames to WinPebble or any other service.

The app saves the output PDF alongside the input file and preserves the
original. Temporary output files are used during processing and cleanup is
attempted when processing finishes or fails. Your PDFs remain under your
control; uninstalling the app does not delete the documents you created.

## Collection and sharing

The app does not collect personal information or usage analytics, use tracking
technologies, show advertisements, or run a background service. It does not
require a WinPebble account or an internet connection to process PDFs.

Microsoft Store and Windows may process information under Microsoft's privacy
policies when you acquire, install, update, or use software on Windows.

## Support

For questions about this policy or the app, use the
[WinPebble PDF Page Duplicator support tracker](https://github.com/WinPebble/pdf-page-duplicator/issues).
GitHub issues are public. Do not include private PDF documents or personal
information in an issue. GitHub handles information you provide there under
its own privacy policy.
